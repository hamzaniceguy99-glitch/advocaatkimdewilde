Privacy policy

We collect the minimum needed to answer you and deliver the coaching. We never sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

What we collect

  • Contact form: name, email address, the program and level you select, and your message.

The only thing stored in your browser is a display preference (light or dark theme). It never reaches us.

  • Delivering coaching: kept for the length of the engagement, then 3 years.
